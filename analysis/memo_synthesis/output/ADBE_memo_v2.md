## AI Thesis — Honest Assessment

### Current State of AI Monetization

| Metric | FY2025 | % of Total | Path to Materiality |
|--------|--------|------------|---------------------|
| AI-first ARR | $250M | 1% of $25.2B ARR | Immaterial currently |
| AI-influenced ARR | ~$8.4B | 33% of ARR | Embedded, not incremental |
| Firefly Generations | 24B cumulative | N/A | Engagement, not revenue |

**The Math Problem:**
- $250M → $500M (FY2026, if 100% growth): Still only 2% of ARR
- $500M → $1B (FY2027): Requires sustained 100% growth
- $1B → $2B (FY2028-2029): 4-5 years to reach 8% of ARR

**For AI to justify a re-rating, Adobe needs ~$2-3B AI ARR (10%+ of total).** At current trajectory, that's 4-5 years away.

### Why "Commercial Warranty" Is Not a Moat

V1 claimed Firefly's licensed training data creates a defensible moat. This was overstated:

1. **Microsoft Copilot** offers commercial indemnification with better distribution (365 suite)
2. **Google** offers enterprise AI indemnification across Workspace
3. **Shutterstock/Getty** have licensed training data to multiple AI providers
4. **Amazon Bedrock** offers enterprise-grade AI with legal coverage

The "commercial warranty" is **table stakes for enterprise AI**, not a differentiator.
