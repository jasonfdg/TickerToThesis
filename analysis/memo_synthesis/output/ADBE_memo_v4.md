## Investment Thesis (2-3 sentences upfront)

Adobe is a wide-moat software franchise with 90%+ retention rates, 89% gross margins, and ~$10B annual FCF — characteristics of a quality compounder. **However, growth has decelerated from 18% CAGR (2013-2020) to 10%, competitors Canva and Figma are aggressively moving upmarket (both claim 95% Fortune 500 penetration), and AI monetization ($250M ARR) remains immaterial at 1% of total revenue.** At $310 (15x forward P/E), the market is correctly pricing in these risks — this is a quality business at fair value, not a mispriced compounder. **Action price: $240 (10x forward P/E).**

### Sustainable Competitive Advantage (Moat Source and Durability)

**Primary Moat: Switching Costs**
- Industry-standard file formats (.PSD, .AI, .PDF) create workflow lock-in
- Creative professionals invest years mastering Adobe tools; retraining costs prohibitive (~$10K+ per employee in productivity loss)
- Enterprise customers locked into multi-year contracts with deep workflow integration
- 90%+ retention rates sustained for 10+ years
- 130%+ net revenue retention demonstrates expansion within existing accounts

**Secondary Moat: Network Effects**
- 50M+ Behance community creates ecosystem stickiness
- Third-party plugins and templates built exclusively for Adobe tools

**Tertiary Moat: Intangible Assets**
- 30+ years of brand equity; "Photoshop" is a verb
- Proprietary AI training data from Adobe Stock (commercially licensed)
- 95% of Fortune 100 companies use Adobe products

### Moat Durability Assessment (Honest Evaluation)

### Why Consensus Is Half-Right (Our Variant View — Developed Through Iteration)

**Where we agree with consensus:** Adobe is fairly valued at $310. The de-rating from 46x to 15x P/E is justified by growth deceleration.

**Where we disagree with consensus:** Analyst targets of $420 are anchored to historical multiples that no longer apply. A 10% grower with increasing competitive pressure should not trade at 18x forward P/E.

**Our unique insight:** The market underestimates switching cost *durability*, not switching cost *existence*. While Canva/Figma have penetrated Fortune 500 headcount, actual revenue displacement will take longer than bears expect due to:

1. **File format lock-in** — .PSD, .AI, .PDF are industry standards; interoperability requires industry-wide coordination
2. **Enterprise contract inertia** — Multi-year renewals with procurement bureaucracy create 2-3 year lag
3. **Retraining costs** — Professional-grade workflow migration costs $10K+ per employee in productivity

This creates a **3-5 year window** where Adobe's FCF engine compounds before competitive pressure fully materializes. At $240 (10x P/E), you're buying this compounding window at a distressed multiple.

**What changed from V1:** We no longer claim the moat is impenetrable. We acknowledge competitive pressure is real. The variant is now about *timing* of pressure, not *existence* of pressure.
