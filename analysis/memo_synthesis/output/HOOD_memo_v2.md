### What We Believe Differently (Specific Claims)

**Claim 1: Distribution is the moat that matters for retail tokenization, and Robinhood has it.**

- Robinhood: 24M funded accounts, 110M+ app downloads, median age 35
- Coinbase: 9.3M monthly active trading users, but focused on crypto-native audience
- Robinhood owns the "first brokerage" relationship for 45% of Gen Z/Millennial investors who started investing in early adulthood

**Why this matters:** Tokenized stocks are a distribution problem, not a technology problem. The tech is commoditized (Arbitrum, Base, Optimism all work). The question is: who can get 100M retail users to custody assets on-chain? Robinhood's existing user base is the answer.

**Evidence:**
- Robinhood's EU tokenized stocks launched to 200+ assets with reported 20,000+ traders and $50M+ volume shortly after launch
- This happened without Robinhood Chain—imagine adoption with native L2 and zero gas fees

**Claim 2: Robinhood Chain solves a different problem than Coinbase Base.**

| Dimension | Coinbase Base | Robinhood Chain |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Target user | Crypto-native, DeFi | Traditional retail investor |
| Primary use case | DeFi protocols, memecoins | Tokenized stocks, RWAs |
| KYC/AML | Optional (permissionless) | Built-in (compliance-first) |
| Current TVL | $11-15B | Not yet live |
| Distribution | 9.3M MAU traders | 24M funded accounts |

Base is winning the L2 war for crypto-native users. But tokenized stocks/RWAs require compliance infrastructure that Base doesn't prioritize. Robinhood Chain's built-in KYC/AML is a feature, not a bug, for institutional RWA issuers.

**Claim 3: The market underestimates switching costs once assets are on-chain.**

Current state: Users can easily switch between Robinhood, Schwab, Fidelity (ACATS transfer).

Future state: If a user's tokenized stocks live on Robinhood Chain, switching requires:
- Bridging assets cross-chain (friction, fees, risk)
- Re-KYC on new platform
- Potential tax events

This creates stickiness that traditional brokerage doesn't have.
